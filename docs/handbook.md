# 1. Introduction

Welcome to ABC Technologies Private Limited!

This Employee Handbook explains the company's policies, rules, responsibilities, and workplace guidelines. All employees are expected to read, understand, and follow these policies.

The purpose of this handbook is to create a professional, safe, productive, and positive working environment.

# 2. Working Hours

1. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
2. Employees must work 8 hours per day.
3. Employees receive a one-hour lunch break.
4. The regular working days are Monday to Friday.
5. Saturday and Sunday are weekly holidays.
6. Employees must inform their managers if they expect to arrive late.
7. Repeated late arrivals may result in disciplinary action.

# 3. Attendance Policy

1. Employees must record their attendance every working day.
2. Attendance must be recorded using the company's attendance system.
3. Employees must inform their reporting manager about unexpected absences.
4. Unauthorized absence is not permitted.
5. Employees working remotely must remain available during working hours.
6. Attendance records are maintained by the HR department.

# 4. Leave Policy

The company provides the following leave benefits:

**Casual Leave**
- Employees receive 12 casual leave days per year.
- Casual leave can be used for personal reasons.
- Employees should request leave at least two days in advance whenever possible.

**Sick Leave**
- Employees receive 10 sick leave days per year.
- Sick leave is provided when employees are unable to work due to illness.
- Medical documentation may be requested for extended sick leave.

**Earned Leave**
- Employees receive 15 earned leave days per year.
- Earned leave should be approved by the reporting manager.
- Unused earned leave may be carried forward according to company policy.

**Emergency Leave**
- Employees can request emergency leave for unexpected situations.
- Employees must inform their manager as soon as possible.

# 5. Salary and Payroll Policy

1. Salaries are paid monthly.
2. Salary payments are processed on the last working day of each month.
3. Employees receive their salaries through bank transfers.
4. Salary slips are provided electronically.
5. Employees must inform HR about changes to their bank account details.
6. Salary deductions may apply according to applicable laws and company policies.
7. Salary-related questions should be directed to the HR department.

# 6. Employee Benefits

The company provides the following employee benefits:

1. Health insurance according to the employee's eligibility.
2. Paid annual leave.
3. Professional development opportunities.
4. Training programs and technical workshops.
5. Performance-based bonuses where applicable.
6. Employee recognition programs.
7. Access to company-provided equipment and software.

# 7. Work From Home Policy

1. Employees may work remotely with manager approval.
2. Employees must remain available during standard working hours.
3. Employees must attend scheduled online meetings.
4. Company data must be accessed through authorized systems.
5. Employees must maintain a secure internet connection.
6. Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.
7. Employees must protect company laptops and other equipment.

# 8. Code of Conduct

All employees must maintain professional behavior.

Employees are expected to:

1. Treat colleagues with respect.
2. Communicate professionally.
3. Avoid discrimination and harassment.
4. Protect company information.
5. Follow company rules and regulations.
6. Avoid conflicts of interest.
7. Maintain honesty and integrity.
8. Use company resources responsibly.

Harassment, bullying, discrimination, and unethical behavior are strictly prohibited.

# 9. IT and Security Policy

1. Employees must use strong passwords.
2. Passwords must never be shared with other employees.
3. Company devices must be used responsibly.
4. Unauthorized software installation is prohibited.
5. Employees must not access restricted company systems.
6. Suspicious emails and security incidents must be reported to the IT department.
7. Company data must not be copied to personal storage without authorization.
8. Employees must lock their computers when away from their desks.

# 10. Holidays Policy

The company observes national and regional public holidays.

The HR department publishes the official holiday calendar at the beginning of each year.

Employees should contact HR for the latest holiday schedule.

# 11. Performance Evaluation

1. Employee performance is reviewed periodically.
2. Managers evaluate employees based on productivity, quality of work, teamwork, and professional development.
3. Employees may receive feedback during performance reviews.
4. Promotions and salary revisions depend on performance and company requirements.
5. Employees are encouraged to improve their technical and professional skills.

# 12. Training and Development

The company encourages continuous learning.

Employees may participate in:

- Technical training sessions.
- Leadership development programs.
- Communication workshops.
- Online certification courses.
- Internal knowledge-sharing sessions.

Employees should discuss their learning goals with their managers.

# 13. Resignation Policy

1. Employees who wish to resign must submit a formal resignation letter.
2. The standard notice period is 30 days, subject to the employment agreement.
3. Employees must complete the assigned handover process.
4. Company assets must be returned before the final working day.
5. HR will process the employee's final settlement according to company policy and applicable laws.
6. Employees will receive their experience and relieving letters subject to clearance requirements.

# 14. Termination Policy

Employment may be terminated for reasons including:

1. Serious misconduct.
2. Repeated violation of company policies.
3. Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
4. Fraudulent activities.
5. Poor performance after appropriate improvement procedures.
6. Organizational restructuring.

All termination procedures must follow applicable employment laws and contractual requirements.

# 15. Employee Grievance Policy

Employees can report workplace concerns or complaints to the HR department.

The company aims to:

1. Listen to employee concerns.
2. Maintain confidentiality wherever possible.
3. Investigate complaints fairly.
4. Take appropriate corrective action.
5. Prevent retaliation against employees who raise genuine concerns.

# 16. Confidentiality Policy

Employees must protect confidential company information.

Confidential information includes:

- Customer information.
- Business strategies.
- Financial records.
- Source code.
- Internal documents.
- Employee personal information.

Employees must not disclose confidential information without proper authorization.
